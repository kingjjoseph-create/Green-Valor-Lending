# Compliance Review Log

> This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice, and it does not show that the site is legally compliant. Writing a page does not make it compliant. Every item below needs review by licensed mortgage compliance counsel, and the site must not launch publicly until the release blockers are cleared.

## Business facts the site enforces

- Green Valor Lending LLC is a **mortgage broker**. It is **not** a direct lender, mortgage bank, or delegated/non-delegated correspondent. It does not underwrite, approve, or fund loans.
- **Florida only.** `licensedStates` contains only FL.
- **Licensing: pending, unverified.** The company NMLS ID is pending, and a pending ID is not an active license. The site prints no NMLS number or license number until they are set, and never prints "Pending" as an ID.
- **No rates.** `features.publishRates` is hard-coded `false`.
- **No approval promises, testimonials, statistics, staff, history, or lender names** are published.

## Release blockers and the publication guard

The rules live in `src/lib/release-blockers.mjs` and read `src/config/compliance-approvals.json` plus the licensing and contact environment variables. Run `npm run launch:check` to list what remains (it exits 1 while anything is open).

| Blocker (from the business's list)                     | Cleared when                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mortgage licensing status remains unverified           | `licenseVerification.status` is `"active"` with `verifiedOn` and `reviewer`, plus NMLS ID and FL license number |
| Required company identification cannot be displayed    | `NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_NMLS_ID` (numeric) and `NEXT_PUBLIC_FL_LICENSE_NUMBER` are set                             |
| Verified company contact details are missing           | `NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_PHONE` and/or `NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_EMAIL` are set                                          |
| Placeholder legal content remains                      | `legalContent` approved (reviewer + date)                                                                       |
| Privacy practices differ from the posted policy        | `privacyPractices` approved                                                                                     |
| Form consent handling is incomplete                    | `formConsent` approved                                                                                          |
| Advertised loan products are not confirmed             | `loanProducts` approved                                                                                         |
| Required disclosures have not been approved            | `disclosures` approved                                                                                          |
| Mortgage advertising review (table below) not complete | `advertisingReview` approved                                                                                    |

An approval counts only with `approved: true`, a named reviewer, and a `YYYY-MM-DD` date. Nothing is pre-approved in the repository (test-enforced).

**While any blocker remains:**

- Every page shows a licensing notice: no mortgage loan applications are taken, and nothing on the site is an offer to arrange a loan.
- `robots.txt` blocks crawling and pages are `noindex`, even if `NEXT_PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING=true`.
- A build with `NEXT_PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING=true` or `LAUNCH_CHECK=strict` **fails** and lists the blockers. Preview builds still succeed.
- The secure application (ARIVE) button is hidden until licensing is verified, even if its URL is set.
- The legal pages carry a "Draft for legal review" banner until `legalContent` is approved. After approval, they show "Effective {date}".

### Copy to review by page

- **Homepage** (`src/content/home.ts`): example programs presented as "may include" with an availability disclaimer; government non-affiliation statement.
- **Residential** (`src/content/residential.ts`): Conventional, FHA and VA descriptions; FAQ answers (preapproval, mortgage insurance, closing costs including Florida documentary stamp and intangible taxes, TRID timing); VA, FHA and HUD non-affiliation. Down-payment amounts and percentages are deliberately left out.
- **Non-QM** (`src/content/non-qm.ts`): Bank Statement, DSCR and 1099 cards, the QM and ability-to-repay explanation, the comparison table, and the FAQ. No thresholds or rates are stated.
- **Resources:** calculator disclaimer and assumptions. A blank, user-entered rate is used, with no default rate. Also the mortgage-basics and FAQ answers.
- **About** (`src/content/about.ts`): veteran-owned statement and values. Before publishing any team profile, confirm the name, title, portrait license and individual NMLS ID, then set `approved: true` in `src/content/team.ts`.
- **Careers** (`src/content/careers.ts`): licensing statement; no guarantee of employment, sponsorship or training placement; growth-card bullets.
- **Contact and Get Started:** consent wording, the "What to expect" note, and the confirmation text.
- **Legal pages** (`src/content/legal/`): Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Accessibility, Consumer Complaints, Licensing.
- **Other Residential Options:** before listing any program, verify its current availability with a wholesale lending partner (`verifiedPrograms` array).

## How to record a sign-off

1. The reviewer confirms the item.
2. Fill in **Reviewer** and **Approval date** in the table above.
3. Set the matching entry in `src/config/compliance-approvals.json` to `approved: true`, with the same reviewer and date. For licensing, set `status: "active"`, `verifiedOn` and `reviewer` after checking NMLS Consumer Access and the Florida OFR license search.
4. Run `npm run launch:check`.
5. Turn on `NEXT_PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING=true` only when it reports no blockers.

## Safeguards

- Publication guard (see above): licensing notice on every page, forced noindex, application link hidden, public builds fail while blockers remain, legal pages marked as drafts (test-enforced).
- Legal pages: the Licensing page shows "Not yet issued" and "Pending; not yet verified" instead of numbers, never treats an issued NMLS ID alone as an active license, and links only the NMLS Consumer Access home page. Regulator links are https on official hosts only. The Privacy Policy is tested against the code: no analytics packages, cookies, or browser storage writes exist, and no encryption, retention-period or certification claims are made (test-enforced).

- Get Started inquiries go only to `/api/inquiry` as `mortgage_inquiry` / `borrower_lead` records. The form is labeled a preliminary inquiry, not a loan application, and asks for no SSN, date of birth, account numbers, tax returns, paystubs, license or credit report; free text that looks like an SSN or account/card number is rejected. Only Florida properties are accepted, and other states get a notice that Green Valor serves Florida only (no out-of-state offer). Consent wording states it does not lock a rate or guarantee approval, and that lending partners make all credit decisions. The ARIVE button renders only for an https URL, and the site loads no analytics. Applicant data is never written to logs, the console or browser storage (test-enforced).

- Contact inquiries go only to `/api/contact`, classified `mortgage_inquiry` (borrower) or `general_inquiry`; career inquiries are refused there and routed to `/careers`. No contact detail renders unless configured, and the address also needs explicit approval (test-enforced).

- Recruitment inquiries go only to `/api/recruitment` and are labeled `recruitment_inquiry` / `careers`, never as borrower leads. Server-side validation, same-origin check, size and rate limits, honeypot, no applicant data in logs, and no success message unless delivery is confirmed (test-enforced).

- `robots.txt` disallows all crawling and pages carry `noindex` until `NEXT_PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING=true`.
- Photography: only licensed, locally stored images; no remote image hosts allowed (test-enforced). See docs/IMAGE_LICENSES.md.
- Homepage copy is test-checked for rates/APR, guarantees, pre-approval, "we fund/approve/underwrite/lend", superlatives, statistics and testimonials, and must keep the "may include" label, the availability disclaimer, and "the lender makes the credit decision".
- Unit tests assert: FL only, two product categories, `publishRates` false, approved brand lines verbatim, no "guaranteed approval" style phrases, broker statement present.
